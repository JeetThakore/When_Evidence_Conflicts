Question-grouped re-evaluation of the CAS conflict detector
(Han, Lan, Kilicoglu, BioNLP 2026). Within-condition detector, six models.

What was run
- Run A: the released pipeline with its instance-level 80/20 split and 5-fold CV.
- Run B: the same pipeline with one change. Every topic_id is placed wholly in train or wholly in test, in the 80/20 split and in the CV on the train part (StratifiedGroupKFold).
- Features, labels, tau = 0.7, alpha = 0.5, the classifier and the coverage targets are unchanged.
- Both modes were run with seeds 0 to 19. Ablations restricted the detector features to uncertainty only, uncertainty plus document embeddings, and question embedding only, for IC and ICC.

Run A reproduces the paper
- The cached results give a six-model mean detector AUROC of 0.718 (IC) and 0.725 (ICC), and a confidence AUROC of 0.478 and 0.655.
- The rerun with seed 42 gives 0.718 and 0.725. Per-model detector AUROC differs from the cached value by at most 0.002 in any condition.
- Selective-prediction accuracy and coverage match exactly in 85 of 90 model, condition and coverage cells. The other 5 differ by 1 to 4 answered test instances at a threshold boundary.
- Over 20 instance-split seeds, the six-model mean detector AUROC is 0.747 ± 0.015 (IC) and 0.716 ± 0.014 (ICC).
- The six-model mean CAS lift over confidence at 75/50/25% coverage is +8.5/+21.0/+33.1 pp (IC) and +3.9/+8.7/+11.5 pp (ICC).

Run B (questions unseen in training)
- In the instance split, about 49 of the 52 test questions also appear in training. In the question split, none do. Test sets hold 15 questions and about 20% of instances on average.
- The six-model mean detector AUROC falls to 0.524 ± 0.021 (IC) and 0.512 ± 0.024 (ICC).
- Raw confidence AUROC on the same test sets is 0.461 (IC) and 0.658 (ICC). In ICC, confidence ranks errors better than the detector.
- The six-model mean CAS lift over confidence at 75/50/25% coverage becomes +0.2/+1.4/+2.9 pp (IC) and -3.1/-1.8/+0.4 pp (ICC).
- The seed SD of these lifts is 1.4 to 3.2 pp, so no IC or ICC lift in the question split is clearly different from zero.
- The pattern holds per model. In IC, phi4-14b and qwen3-4b show negative lifts at every coverage level (-6.6 to -14.3 pp). qwen3.5-9b keeps a positive lift (+7.8 to +13.2 pp).
- The other conditions show the same direction. Detector AUROC drops from 0.904 to 0.656 (NC), 0.714 to 0.494 (CC) and 0.693 to 0.536 (CIC).

Ablations (detector AUROC, six-model mean, instance vs question)
- Question embedding only: 0.780 vs 0.594 (IC), 0.767 vs 0.450 (ICC). In the instance split this single feature block beats the full detector (0.747, 0.716).
- Uncertainty plus document embeddings: 0.699 vs 0.533 (IC), 0.673 vs 0.532 (ICC).
- Uncertainty only: 0.538 vs 0.563 (IC), 0.533 vs 0.499 (ICC). This is the only feature set that does not drop in the question split.
- In the question split, the uncertainty-only detector gives a larger IC CAS lift (+2.6/+6.3/+13.7 pp) than the full detector (+0.2/+1.4/+2.9 pp).

Answer to the question
- On questions the detector has never seen, CAS does not beat confidence in IC or ICC by a margin larger than the seed variation.
- The gains in Run A appear to come mostly from recognising questions that recur in training. The question-embedding-only result supports this.
- An instance-level split may have been intended, for example if the target use case is a fixed question set. In that case the paper's numbers are correct for that setting. They do not describe transfer to new questions.

Caveats and other observations
1. The released config.py does not define HC_CONFIDENTLY_WRONG_THRESHOLD or HC_CROSS_CONDITION_DELTA_THRESHOLD. The released code fails at startup without them. They were added with the values 0.7 and 0.3. The value 0.7 matches the paper. The value 0.3 matches delta_threshold in the cached JSONs. With these values Run A matches the cached results.
2. In the threshold-transfer protocol, the confidence-only baseline keeps every test instance whose confidence is at or above the k-th highest value. Because many confidences are tied near 1.0, the baseline answers more instances than CAS. At the 25% IC target, CAS answers 25.1% of the test set and confidence answers 40.7%. A matched-coverage check using the exact top-k results that the same code writes gives the same conclusion: IC lift +8.5/+21.0/+33.3 pp (instance) vs +2.4/+0.9/+2.0 pp (question), ICC lift +3.6/+8.4/+10.9 pp vs -4.2/-0.5/+1.8 pp.
3. With question groups, the cross-condition detector (out of scope here) sometimes had too few positives for a stratified group split. It fell back to GroupShuffleSplit, and some CV folds had a single class. A guard now gives such folds a constant score in question mode only. The within-condition detector produced no degenerate splits in any of the 600 model, condition and seed combinations per mode.
4. Question-split test sets hold about 15 questions, so results vary more across seeds. Topic 154 alone holds 77 of the 920 instances. All values are reported as mean ± SD over 20 seeds.
5. Library versions: Python 3.11, scikit-learn 1.9.1, sentence-transformers 6.1.0, torch 2.14.1 (CPU).
